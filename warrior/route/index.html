Cash the bank first with the ledger’s cap-release turn-in route; it ends in Stormwind, where block A starts. The quests you hold in the ledger are your bank: their XP is the starting point here, and the matching Duskwood and Wetlands quests below are struck through. The plan then adds every planned quest in route order, applies Forever’s low-level rule at the level you will be at, and adds estimated kill XP. Tick quests as you play. Without a level override, ticked quests count as XP earned on the route; once you set your current level, that level is your XP and ticked quests leave the plan.

Saving and the rules behind the numbers

This browser only

Route ticks and settings are saved in this browser under their own key; the ledger is only read, never changed. Export a JSON file to move them to another device.

  • Levels follow the Classic curve from level 20: 21 at 23,200 · 22 at 48,400 · 23 at 75,700 · 24 at 105,100 · 25 at 136,800 · 26 at 170,800 · 27 at 207,200 · 28 at 246,100 · 29 at 287,500 · 30 at 331,800.
  • Low-level rule (player-reported; check it on the first reduced turn-in): a quest pays 100% while you are at most five levels above its level, 80% at six, 60% at seven and 50% from eight. Reduced rows read “1,250 → 750 at 28”.
  • Dungeon quests pay the beta multiple of Wowhead’s value: 3.75× in Blackfathom Deeps, 3.2× in the Stockade, about 2.6× in Gnomeregan.
  • Kill XP is an estimate: about 40% of the quest XP while questing, scaled to the share of each block’s quests still planned, plus a fixed figure for each dungeon run.
  • Route scope. The route says: with about 40,000 banked, drop the optional quests in blocks B and C; with about 80,000, use Cybes Dududu’s shortlists. Auto applies that; any quest can still be added or skipped by hand. If behind quests join only when the block would end below its leave level.
  • Bank XP is the ledger’s listed XP for every quest held, without the low-level rule; a level-17 or level-18 quest turned in late pays less, so set your current level once the bank is cashed.
