40-slot quest bank

Set a quest to Ready only when its objectives are complete and it is waiting in your journal. Ready quests drive the live slot and XP totals.

You are capped at 20: hold every completed bank quest. From now until the cap rises, any XP from a turn-in is wasted. Complete quests and keep them in the log; turn in only prerequisites that unlock another bank quest (their own XP is lost, so do it only when the follow-up is worth more). Mob-kill XP is wasted too, so spend the time on completions, gear, and Blacksmithing.
Protect these before the cap increase: The Defias Brotherhood (Chausses of Westfall), Underground Assault, The Unsent Letter, and Bloodied Insignia (Remembrance Armor). Their rewards and follow-ups wait until after the mass turn-in.
Flags: needs 20 the quest is reported to start at level 20. hard at 20 the targets are level 24–28 elites or packs; bring a full group or skip them. decay risk a level-17 or level-18 quest that pays less once you are six or more levels above it; turn it in early on release night. Minimum levels for most Redridge, Duskwood, and Wetlands quests were not confirmed: if the quest giver does not offer one, it needs a higher level.

Practical Protection set

One-hander and shield, Alliance-accessible sources only. Stamina and armor first, then Defense, then Strength. No Merchant’s Favor is needed for the core list.

Stat rule for a level-20 tank: Stamina and armor keep the healer comfortable; Defense lowers the chance to be crit and raises avoidance; Strength adds threat and block. Agility gives armor and dodge, so it is a fine secondary stat. Weapon damage still matters for threat, but a shield always beats a two-hander while tanking.
The shield: Kresh’s Back (528 armor, +5 Defense, Wailing Caverns) is the clear level-18 target. Kresh is the turtle in the lake area; ask the group to kill him.

Mining and Blacksmithing

Self-crafted tank pieces and weapon stones within reach before and just after level 20. This is not a power-leveling plan: mine nodes on your route and craft at natural breaks.

Best value: Gemmed Copper Boots (Blacksmithing 35, 109 armor, +4 Strength, +4 Stamina) are mail, cheap, and good until 20. Rough Bronze Shoulders fill an otherwise empty slot from level 17. Weapon stones are a cheap threat boost for every dungeon pull.
Learn ranks: the client data shows each recipe’s colour range but not every trainer learn rank. Check the trainer before buying materials.

Cap-release turn-in

Bind the hearthstone in Stormwind. If you hold the Wailing Caverns quests, log out in Ratchet and start there; otherwise start in Stormwind. Order: Barrens → (Menethil if Wetlands quests are held) → Stormwind, first half → Sentinel Hill and the Deadmines exit → Darkshire → Lakeshire → Tower of Azora → Stormwind, second half. Skip any stop where you hold nothing.

Before clicking anything: read the live reward pane. Forever is in beta, and the live journal overrides this ledger’s saved research. The level-17 and level-18 quests come early in this order so they pay full XP.

XP threshold once you are 20

For a completed quest with no follow-up:

≤ 1,500 XP
Turn it in while leveling. It is below the practical replacement line.
1,550–1,950
Usually turn in. Hold only when already complete near 20, on the route, or needed to fill a slot.
2,000–2,450
Check the bank before turning in. This is normal bank-candidate territory.
≥ 2,500 XP
Preserve by default unless the turn-in unlocks a stronger chain or an urgent upgrade.

Below 20 the rule is simpler: XP is never wasted, so turn in anything that helps you reach 20 before the release. Quest chains override the threshold. Turn in The Valor Family, the first two Night Watch stages, and the Redridge prerequisites because they unlock stronger bank targets.

Low-level XP reduction: a quest pays 80% once you are six levels above it, 60% at seven, and 50% from eight (player-reported). Only the six decay risk quests here can be affected, and only if you turn them in after passing level 23–24.

Travel value

  • Wailing Caverns: worth one full run for Kresh’s Back alone, plus five bank quests if you finish through Mutanus before the release. Holding them means logging out in Ratchet.
  • The Deadmines: the core run. Cruel Barb, First Mate Band, and Blackened Defias Armor all drop here, and it carries seven bank quests.
  • Ruins of Lordaeron: go when a group already wants it. The three Stormwind hand-ins are excellent; Abominable Creatures turns in inside the dungeon and gives the Slain Baron’s Signet tank ring on the spot.
  • Duskwood: best open-world follow-up. The western spiders and wolves are fine solo with a shield; Totem, Silvia, Merrick, and final Night Watch need a partner.
  • Hall of Thanes: a level 13–18 dungeon. Worth one run only if a group is going: several reward pieces in the gear list come from it.
  • Shadowfang Keep and Blackfathom Deeps: after the cap rises. Blackfathom Villainy offers the Arctic Buckler shield.

Protection talents at 20

A dedicated level-20 tank build is 0/0/11 Protection: 5/5 Shield Specialization, 2/2 Improved Bloodrage, 3/3 Improved Thunder Clap, and 1/3 Improved Revenge. At 18 you have 9 points.

Pull: Charge in Battle Stance, swap to Defensive Stance, then Bloodrage, Shield Block, Revenge whenever lit, Thunder Clap on packs, Demoralizing Shout once, Sunder Armor on bosses, Heroic Strike only with spare Rage, and Taunt only after losing threat.

Visit the Warrior trainer at 20 before the next dungeon if you have not already.
